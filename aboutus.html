About us

Arya Properties Investment Ltd is a privately owned property investment company incorporated in England and Wales

The company focuses on residential property investment and the long-term ownership and management of residential assets in the United Kingdom.

Our investment strategy is centred on identifying suitable residential properties in established locations, acquiring properties with sound investment fundamentals and managing them responsibly over the long term.

We aim to maintain our properties to a good standard and provide suitable accommodation for our tenants.

Our Investment Philosophy

Our approach is based on four principles:

Long-Term Investment

We focus on building sustainable value through long-term property ownership rather than short-term speculation.

Prudent Financial Management

We take a considered approach to property acquisition, financing and ongoing portfolio management.

Quality Property

We seek to maintain our properties to a good standard and undertake appropriate maintenance and improvements.

Professional Relationships

We value long-term relationships with lenders, professional advisers, letting agents, contractors and other business partners.
